How can we help you?

Welcome to the RoadFix LK Help Center. Find answers to common questions and learn how to use the platform effectively.

For Citizens (Reporters)

1. Ensure you are logged into your account.
2. Click on the "Report Issue" button in the top navigation bar.
3. Drag the map marker to precisely select the location of the road damage.
4. Upload a clear photograph of the damage by dragging it into the upload box.
5. Select the damage type and severity, and provide any additional description or landmarks.
6. Click "Submit Report". Our AI will automatically analyze your image!

You can track the status of your reports at any time by navigating to the "My Reports" page. Click on the report ID to view a detailed timeline of actions taken by the authorities. You will also receive in-app notifications when the status of your report changes.

To keep our system efficient, we automatically detect duplicate reports. If someone else has already reported the exact same issue in the same location, your report will be linked to theirs. This increases the "Duplicate Count" of the original report, which instantly raises its Priority Score, ensuring authorities fix it faster!
For Authorities (Officers & Admins)

Navigate to the "Manage Reports" page. You will see a list of all active reports, automatically sorted by their AI-calculated Priority Score. You can use the filter tabs at the top to view reports by their current status (New, Verified, Assigned, etc.).

Reports follow a strict pipeline to ensure accountability:

New → Verified → Assigned → Under Repair → Completed → Resolved (Confirmed)

Note: Only the citizen who originally reported the issue can move it from "Completed" to "Resolved" after verifying the repair in person.

The Priority Score (ranging from 0.0 to 100.0) is automatically calculated by the system to help authorities decide which issues to fix first. It factors in:
  • Severity: Critical issues receive higher base scores.
  • Duplicate Count: Issues reported by multiple citizens receive significant score boosts.
  • Age: Reports that have been open for a long time slowly gain priority so they aren't forgotten.
